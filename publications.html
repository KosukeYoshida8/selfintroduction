Publications

Selected papers and preprints.

Peer-reviewed

  1. Wada R., Tomatsuri T., Kosuke Y., Hakataya S., Toya G., Okanoya K., & Koda H. (2025). Rats can spontaneously discriminate different rhythms: an investigation using an automated habituation-dishabituation paradigm. Behavioural Processes, 105257.
    Description: This study used an automated habituation-dishabituation paradigm to test whether rats can spontaneously discriminate rhythmic patterns, providing behavioral evidence for rhythm sensitivity in rodents.
