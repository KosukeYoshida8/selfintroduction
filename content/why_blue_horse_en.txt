This expressionisitc painting is "Blue Horse" by Franz Marc. 
This painting inspired young Eric Carle, who created "The Very Hungry Caterpillar", sparking his lifelong passion 
for artistic freedom such that you can draw a horse blue. 

I love this story and am fascinated by the human's limitless imagination, as it gives us 
the ultimate freedom to let our thoughts run wild in our mind’s eye
