About

A brief academic biography and research background.

Career

Hi, I am Kosuke, a PhD student working on cognitive neuroscience, especially social cognition. My background combines theory and experimental approaches. I am interested in how the brain processes social information and leads to decision making. I model the animal behavoirs and underlying cognitive processes using computational and statistical methods. I'm working on neural recordings too such as fMRI and Ultrasound Imaging.
